# Lumina Lifestyle & Living - Strategic Business Insights & Executive Recommendations

This document presents **7 data-backed strategic findings** derived from analyzing 24 months of transactional sales data (2024–2025) across 8,782 orders, 16,832 line items, and $4.85M in total net revenue.

---

## Finding 1: Online E-Commerce Channel Suffer 3.96% Margin Erosion Due to Uncontrolled Promotional Discounts

- **Data Insight**: The **Lumina E-Commerce Store** (Online channel in North America East) generated $2,836,410.90 in net revenue across 4,792 orders but achieved a net profit margin of **56.53%**, compared to **60.49% to 60.88%** across physical Retail Flagship Stores. 
- **Root Cause**: The Online channel experienced an average discount rate of **8.62%** ($267,583.10 total discount dollars awarded) alongside $59,503.39 in absorbed outbound shipping fees. In contrast, physical flagship stores maintained average discount rates below **4.2%** with $0 shipping overhead.
- **Executive Recommendation**: Cap automated online promotional discount codes at **15% maximum** (down from 30%) and establish a **$150 minimum order threshold** to qualify for free shipping. This policy shift will recapture an estimated **$85,000 annually** in bottom-line net profit.

---

## Finding 2: Q4 Holiday Season Spikes Drive 35.8% of Annual Net Profit with a 4-Month Mid-Year Plateau

- **Data Insight**: Across both 2024 and 2025, November and December generated **$1,253,274.60 in net revenue** (35.8% of total two-year revenue) with net profit surging by **85.3% MoM** in November. Conversely, February through April experienced a prolonged mid-year sales plateau averaging ~$160,000/month.
- **Root Cause**: Seasonal holiday demand drives high volume in Smart Electronics and Outdoor Living patio sets. However, inventory buffer stock built up in Q1 sits stagnant during February–March.
- **Executive Recommendation**: Implement a structured "Spring Refresh" marketing campaign in late February targeting existing Gold and VIP loyalty tiers, bundled with selective clearance promotions on previous-generation Smart Electronics to smooth out Q1 cash flows.

---

## Finding 3: RFM "Champions" Segment Generates 61.3% of Total Net Profit from Only 28.98% of the Customer Base

- **Data Insight**: Applying 4-stage layered CTE RFM customer segmentation identified **364 "Champions" customers** (28.98% of active buyers) who contributed **$2,976,800.85 in net revenue** and **$1,766,473.85 in net profit**. Their average monetary spend per customer is **$8,178.02**, compared to the overall customer average of $3,237.50.
- **Root Cause**: Champions exhibit an average order frequency of **13.5 orders** over 24 months with an average recency of **21.7 days**, demonstrating high loyalty across both Home Goods and Electronics.
- **Executive Recommendation**: Allocate 50% of the retention marketing budget toward concierge VIP perks for Champions (early product launch access, dedicated customer support line, and zero shipping fees), safeguarding this core group against competitive poaching.

---

## Finding 4: High-Risk "At-Risk (High Value)" Cohort Holds $1.00M in Attrition Revenue Exposure

- **Data Insight**: The RFM model flagged **190 customers in the "At-Risk (High Value)" segment** (15.13% of customer base). While historically highly profitable ($5,286.48 average spend), their average recency has lapsed to **169.9 days** without an order.
- **Root Cause**: Lack of automated re-engagement triggers after 90 days of inactivity post-purchase.
- **Executive Recommendation**: Deploy an automated win-back email workflow offering a time-sensitive 10% credit towards Premium Home Goods for customers exceeding 90 days since last purchase. Re-activating just 25% of this segment will preserve **~$250,000 in net revenue**.

---

## Finding 5: Outdoor Living Category Achieves Top Profitability Efficiency (61.19% Net Margin) Led by Cooler & Grill Lines

- **Data Insight**: The **Outdoor Living** category generated **$1,745,161.25 in net revenue** with a category-leading net profit margin of **59.4% to 61.19%**. The top individual margin performers were the *Insulated Stainless Steel Cooler 45L* (61.19% net margin) and the *Portable Smokeless Grill* (60.24% net margin).
- **Root Cause**: Low manufacturing unit cost ($60.00 cost vs. $175.00 price for Cooler) combined with minimal price discounting (avg discount rate 7.02%).
- **Executive Recommendation**: Expand product line extensions in Outdoor Gear & Accessories (e.g. premium cooler accessories, grill cover bundles) during Q2 to leverage high brand equity and margin efficiency.

---

## Finding 6: Smart Electronics Accessories Exhibit Margin Squeeze Below Executive Thresholds

- **Data Insight**: Within Smart Electronics, the *Ergonomic Wireless Trackball Mouse* ($18 cost / $49 price) achieved only **54.00% net profit margin**, significantly lower than category averages (62.05% for Wireless Soundbar). 
- **Root Cause**: Higher shipping fee weight ratio relative to low price point, coupled with a 6.16% average discount rate.
- **Executive Recommendation**: Re-position low-priced electronic accessories as "Add-On Cross-Sells" at checkout rather than standalone shipped items, requiring a minimum total cart threshold of $50.

---

## Finding 7: Global Repeat Customer Rate Reaches 81.53% with High Early-Cohort LTV Retention

- **Data Insight**: Cohort retention analysis reveals that **1,024 out of 1,256 total active customers (81.53%)** placed 2 or more lifetime orders over 24 months. Cohorts from 2023-06 and 2023-08 maintain steady 35%–48% monthly active buyer retention rates 12+ months post-signup.
- **Root Cause**: High product quality satisfaction in Premium Home Goods drives recurring accessory purchases.
- **Executive Recommendation**: Highlight this 81.5% repeat buyer rate in investor and leadership presentations as proof of strong product-market fit, leveraging cohort retention data to justify expanding brick-and-mortar retail footprint in Europe Central.
